**Scope:** Only the track pieces visibly present or clearly required by the complex multi-loop, multi-level blue track layout in the original meme video (top-down view of white bullet train derailing, bouncing across the layout, then recovering). Stationary trains visible at the top of the frame are disregarded.

## Moving Train Only (the one that derails and recovers)

**Description:** Single white streamlined bullet-train / Shinkansen-style locomotive (or short consist) that is actively moving, derails, bounces around the layout, and eventually returns to the rails. Plain white body, blue accents on the nose/cockpit area, three headlights visible. Stationary trains parked at the top of the frame are ignored.

This is a typical Plarail-compatible white Shinkansen body (often N700-series inspired or a generic/“White Stream”-style / Chinese Plarail-compatible clone). Exact model is difficult to pin to one official SKU because many plain white versions and clones exist; the key visual is the clean white streamlined shell with blue nose details.

**Useful visual references for the moving train only:**
- Search product photos for “プラレール ホワイト” or “S-20 ホワイトストリーム” or plain white N700-style single cars.
- Official examples of similar white bodies: Takara Tomy S-series white Shinkansen listings and the “White Stream” (S-20) product pages.
- Generic Plarail-compatible white bullet trains appear frequently on Amazon / eBay / Japanese auction sites under “white Shinkansen Plarail” or “white bullet train toy”.

For simulation purposes, model only this single moving unit (ignore any parked consists).

## 1. Standard Curve Rail
**Code:** R-03  
**Japanese:** 曲線レール  
**Description:** Basic 45° curve. Eight pieces form a full circle. Radius ≈ 215 mm. Primary building block of the many loops and arcs visible in the video.

**Visual references (isolated / packaging style):**

- Product listing examples: Search “プラレール R-03” on Takara Tomy Mall or Yodobashi

## 2. Straight Rail
**Code:** R-01  
**Japanese:** 直線レール  
**Description:** Full-length straight (≈ 215–220 mm). Used for connecting curves into ovals, longer runs, and elevated sections.

**Visual references:**
- Official lineup: https://www.takaratomy.co.jp/products/plarail/lineup/rail/
- Common in every basic set contents diagram (see also the clean set-contents style image you provided earlier)

## 3. Half Straight Rail
**Code:** R-02  
**Japanese:** 1/2直線レール  
**Description:** Half-length straight. Used for length adjustments, gender matching, and in combination with points (especially the R-14 cross).

**Visual references:**

## 4. Turnout Rail (Left + Right)
**Code:** R-11  
**Japanese:** ターンアウトレール (L・R各1本入)  
**Description:** Standard diverging switch. Straight main path with a curved branch (same radius as R-03). Yellow lever. Essential for the branching paths that create the chaotic multi-route layout.

**Visual references (clean isolated photos):**
- Detailed fan page with historical + modern photos: https://www.fallinsideahole.com/collections/plarail/accessories/rail/r11turnout.html
- Official product: Search “プラレール R-11” on Takara Tomy Mall / Yodobashi
- Layout usage examples: Takara Tomy Concierge plans that use turnouts

## 5. Figure-8 / Y-Point Rail (Left + Right)
**Code:** R-12  
**Japanese:** 8の字ポイントレール (L・R各1本入)  
**Description:** Curved diverging point that splits left and right. Designed for figure-8 layouts. Matches the curved branch geometry of standard curves. Highly likely present given the multiple interlocking loops.

**Visual references:**

## 6. Crossing / Cross Point Rail
**Code:** R-14  
**Japanese:** 交差ポイントレール (1/2直線レール4本付)  
**Description:** Flat 4-way cross junction with switchable points (yellow levers). Allows trains to go straight across or divert. The tighter internal curves match the “tighter curve / 4-way cross” components noted as missing from basic sets. Strongly matches the dense junction areas visible in the video.

## 7. Large / Shallow Curve Rail
**Code:** R-09 (sim label; product families vary — “large curve” / 大曲線 / wider-radius 45° arcs)  
**Description:** Shallower 45° curve with approximately **2×** the standard R-03 radius. Needed for sweeping outer loops.

---

## 8. Quarter / 90° Curve (long sharper turn)
**Code:** R-90 (sim)  
**Description:** Single **90°** arc at standard radius — one piece forms a full right-angle turn (equivalent length/shape to two R-03 in series, but as one rigid piece). Matches the long continuous corner arcs in the layout photo that read as sharp quarter turns rather than gentle multi-segment sweeps.

---

## 9. Long Straight + Stop / Bump Straight
**Codes:** R-L (2× straight), R-Stop (2× with lateral station-style bump-out)  
**Description:** Longer straights appear in the photo (grey segment, blue run under the train area, top span between a cross and a Y). One variant has a **side bump-out** (platform / stop geometry) that matters for exterior wall gliding. Sim models length + bump shape only (no colors / scenery).

---

## 10. Three-Way Y-Split (1→3 Point)
**Code:** Y-3 (sim)  
**Description:** **One** stem in, **three** paths out (or reverse). Outer left and outer right exit headings are **90° apart** (±45° from the centerline). Yellow lever cycles left / center / right. Distinct from R-12 (only two curved legs).

---

## 11. Cross with corner webbing
**Code:** R-14  
**Description:** 4-way cross. **Rounded plastic webbing in the four inner corners** is required so an off-rail train can glide around the junction interior edges (as in the meme recover path)—not just bare crossing lines.

---

## Simulation physics note — wheelbase
The engine’s **front guiding wheels sit about ⅓ of the body length back from the nose**. Path following and wall contact use this front-axle point (short wheelbase), so the body leaves curves and slides edges at a **shallower angle** than a center-or-nose contact model.

## Notes for Simulation / Scale Accuracy
- Standard length unit: ≈ 215 mm (straight or curve radius).
- All listed pieces are fully compatible with each other.
- Most are reversible.
- The R-14 is the piece that introduces the tighter internal curves you observed.
- The dense, chaotic appearance comes from heavy use of R-11 + R-12 + R-14 combined with many R-03 curves and elevation.

This document intentionally excludes any Plarail components not required or visible in the meme video layout (double-track rails, special auto-points, U-turn rails, scenery stations, tunnels, etc.).

Sources are current as of August 2026 and prioritise official or high-resolution isolated product photography / diagrams.
