# Plarail Rail Components R-01 to R-17

**Dimensional Reference System**  
The entire Plarail track system is built around one base unit:

- **1 unit = length of R-01 Straight Rail ≈ 216 mm** (official centre-line length; real pieces typically measure 214–216 mm).
- Standard curve radius (R-03) = **1 unit** (215–216 mm to centre line).
- 8 × R-03 = full circle (outer diameter ≈ 470 mm).
- Half straight (R-02) ≈ **0.5 unit**.
- Most points and specials are designed so their overall footprint fits into 1 × 1 unit squares or simple combinations of curves + straights.

This makes layout planning and later digital simulation straightforward.

All modern blue rails are reversible (usable flipped). Connector system is male/female (凸/凹).

Sources: Official Takara Tomy lineup, parlorfleur-pm.com, fallinsideahole.com, and product measurements.

### Notes on R-17 Code Re-use
Takara Tomy re-used the R-17 number in 2025. The older three-way point was discontinued and replaced by the new “Bridge Straight Rail” (橋直線レール). Both are documented above for completeness.
