Controls

  • Left-drag from palette / pieces — magnetic snap when open ends are near. Left-click empty does not spawn pieces.
  • Right-click rail — place train · right-click piece — rotate · right-click empty with a palette piece selected — stamp · yellow lever — switch.
  • Save JSON / Load JSON — export or restore your layout (native .json format).

Pieces

  • Straights: R-01, R-L (2×), R-Stop (bump), R-02
  • Curves: R-03 45°, R-90 quarter, R-09 large/shallow
  • Points: R-11, R-12, Y-3 (1→3, outers 90°), R-14 with corner webbing

Physics note

The train’s front axle sits about ⅓ of the body length back from the nose. Path following and wall glides use that short wheelbase so exits from curves are shallower than a center-point model.
